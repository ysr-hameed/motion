# Social formats

## The hook contract

- Frame 1 ALREADY shows the hook (product, claim, or face). No empty stage, no 2s logo fade-in. The swipe decision happens in ~15f.
- One idea per beat. Punch-in emphasis (scale 1.08 overshoot) on the CTA.
- Resolve BEFORE the platform cap; end card holds ≥30f with the CTA settled and breathing.

## Vertical-safe on a landscape canvas

Action inside the middle 1080px even on 1920×1080 — the 9:16 crop must keep the hero, the CTA, and the captions. State the crop in the plan; the validator can't see it, the audit measures the full frame.

## Sound-off default

Most feeds play muted: captions ON (see `captions`), kinetic type carrying the message, visual punch replacing the drop. If the cut only works with sound, it doesn't work.

## Pacing grammar (5s promo)

| Frames | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–15 | Hook lands (anticipation + punch, product visible frame 1) |
| 15–75 | Proof cascade (3 claims max, staggered, one accent) |
| 75–120 | CTA punch + resolve (scale 1.08, hold, breathe) |
| 120–150 | End card (logo + CTA settled, badge held) |

## Platform notes

- Reels/Shorts/TikTok: 9:16-safe, captions on, resolve before swipe (~5-7s optimal).
- Feed square: center-safe, CTA readable at thumbnail size (hero ≥130px).
- YouTube: native 16:9, first 15f decide retention — hook, don't intro.

## Rules

- ✅ Hook frame 1. ✅ 3 claims max. ✅ CTA punch + 30f hold. ✅ Captions on.
- ❌ Cold opens. ❌ Feature lists (5+ bullets = nobody reads). ❌ CTA that appears in the last 10f.
